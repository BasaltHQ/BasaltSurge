# Workspace Tour

Take The Tour is your personal guide to the admin workspace. Find it in **General → Take The Tour**.

When you start or resume, Daniel, your AI guide, welcomes you and asks what you’d like to be called. Enter a first name or nickname, then choose voice or text in the guide. He’ll greet you personally, introduce your workspace’s tour, and use your name naturally as you explore. This does not change your account profile.

Choose **Brief** for purpose, a useful workflow, and a takeaway for each panel. Choose **Extended** for a deeper walkthrough and examples. Both modes cover your accessible sidebar sections. The guide pauses for questions after every panel and waits for you to continue.

The walkthrough opens each main view and uses translucent boxes to point out controls and content. The AI guide explains each highlight, then automatically moves to the next within that panel. Ask a question at any time; the walkthrough resumes after the answer. Use **Pause walkthrough** for a longer pause and **Resume walkthrough** when ready, or use **Show me** and **Next highlight** yourself. After the final explanation, the guide invites questions and enables **Continue**. Unavailable views and highlights you skip are identified clearly.

These panel guides power the written tour and the AI trainer. The tour selects guides from the sections available to your account; the documentation library itself describes the wider product. A guide never grants access to a panel.

Developers can [add a panel guide](/developers/docs/tour/authoring) by adding a Markdown document. The list below is discovered automatically.
