# Industry Packs & Sidebar Apps

This guide explains how **Industry Packs** customize your BasaltSurge dashboard, enabling vertical-specific sidebar tabs and applications under the **Apps** navigation category.

---

## 1. What are Industry Packs?

Industry Packs are pre-configured templates that adapt the user interface and functionality of your storefront, inventory, and operations to match specific business models. 

Merchants access these custom tools directly under the **Apps** section in the Admin Sidebar. The available options adapt automatically to the active pack.

---

## 2. Restaurant Pack

Designed for food and beverage outlets, quick-service cafes, and full-service dining. When active, it unlocks these sidebar apps:

### A. Kitchen Display System (KDS)
*   **Purpose**: Replaces paper kitchen tickets with a digital dashboard.
*   **Features**:
    *   *Incoming Orders*: Displays paid tickets instantly.
    *   *Prep Stations*: Filter orders by kitchen station (e.g., Grill, Cold, Bar).
    *   *Status Tracking*: Tap orders to mark them as *In Prep* or *Ready for Pickup*.

### B. Table Management
*   **Purpose**: Manage in-person floor layouts.
*   **Features**:
    *   Design a visual grid matching your tables and seating arrangement.
    *   Assign orders to specific tables and track active guest balances.
    *   Split checks or transfer tabs between tables seamlessly.

### C. Delivery Dispatch
*   **Purpose**: Coordinate driver dispatches.
*   **Features**:
    *   Assign completed orders to delivery drivers.
    *   Monitor active delivery queues and driver requests.

---

## 3. Hotel Pack

Tailored for boutique hotels, lodges, and hospitality environments.

### A. Property Management System (PMS) Panel
*   **Purpose**: Connect room check-ins with billing.
*   **Features**:
    *   *Guest Profiles*: Manage check-in, check-out dates, and room assignments.
    *   *Housekeeping*: Monitor room cleanup statuses (Dirty, Clean, Inspect).
    *   *Room Folios*: Charge food, drinks, or spa purchases directly to a guest's room bill, consolidating checkout payments in a single transaction.

---

## 4. Publishing Pack

Designed for book publishers, authors, and self-publishing platforms.

### A. Writer's Workshop Panel
*   **Purpose**: Manage creative content drafts and author details.
*   **Features**:
    *   Submit and organize book manuscripts.
    *   Assign editor review states.

### B. Publications Panel
*   **Purpose**: Register book titles and royalty structures.
*   **Features**:
    *   Track Osiris USBN metadata (a blockchain-based ISBN replacement).
    *   Define on-chain **Royalty Splits**: Set up payment splits that distribute sales proceeds directly to authors, illustrators, and publishers.

---

## 5. Cannabis Pack

Built for dispensaries and highly regulated cannabis retail.

### A. Compliance Panel
*   **Purpose**: Meet regulatory reporting mandates.
*   **Features**:
    *   *Seed-to-Sale Tracking*: Log batches and product categories.
    *   *METRC Sync*: Automate state compliance data synchronization.
    *   *Transfer Manifests*: Generate shipping and compliance manifests.
