# Merchant User Manual (BasaltSurge)

Welcome to the **BasaltSurge Merchant User Manual**. This guide is designed for business owners, store managers, and staff to configure their digital shop front, manage inventories, set up point-of-sale terminals, audit tax compliance, and cash out on-chain earnings.

> [!NOTE]
> **Dynamic Branding Revisions**: 
> BasaltSurge is a white-label platform. Depending on the configured brand key (`brandKey`), the branding elements (logos, colors, site titles, and URLs) will automatically customize to fit your partner network's identity. If your console features a customized name, these instructions apply identically.

---

## Onboarding Checklist

To begin accepting payments, follow these key steps in the admin dashboard:

1. **Connect Your Wallet**: Use the connection button in the navbar to authenticate with your administrator web3 wallet.
2. **Configure Your Profile**: Claim your custom store URL slug and select your role (Merchant or both Buyer/Merchant).
3. **Deploy Payment Splitter**: Ensure a split contract is bound to your account so you can route payments on-chain.
4. **Set Up Inventory**: Upload products, price them in USD, and define customized modifiers.
5. **Manage Taxes**: Add tax jurisdictions and activate components.
6. **Deploy Touchpoints**: Launch public shop interfaces, kiosks, and kitchen display terminals.
